# Rush, first-time player review (build 79, web, 390x780 phone viewport)

**Score: 7/10**

## How far I got
Four services, no losses. I cleared every ticket in services 3 and 4 with an A+ (11 of 11 out, 0 walks, 100 points). Services 1 and 2 went fine too. In the shop I bought Server Tray, Family Meal, Temp Cook (twice), Cast Iron and Heat Lamps. Twists I saw: a critic in service 2 and a VIP table in service 4. I stopped at the start of service 5 with $147 in the bank.

## What hooked me
- "XCOM, but the tickets are the enemy" comes across within two turns. Planning three moves per cook, then hitting END TURN and watching the clock tick, felt good.
- The buttons tell you what will happen: "DEV: FIRE T30", "JO: RUN 2 PLATES", "END TURN / FRY BURNS", "PLATE NOW", and WALK 1 / TOO FAR above each station. Most of the time I knew what to tap without reading the help.
- "TOO FAR: JO NEEDS 4 MOVES, HAS 3" was a clear, fair no.
- The shop, plus a new station opening each service (saute, then cold), gave me a reason to keep going. Buying a tray and seeing "RUN 2 PLATES" felt like a real upgrade.

## What confused me
- Service 1, turn 1: I wasn't sure whether tapping a cook's face or the station is how you pick someone. Tapping the station picks whoever is standing there, so I sent the wrong cook a couple of times before it clicked.
- I couldn't always tell who had moves left. The small dots above the portraits are easy to miss, and "NO MOVES" only shows once you've picked that cook.
- Rows like "at GRILL" vs "cooking" vs "on the pass" on the tickets are good, but on a crowded rail (four tickets plus "+1 MORE") the text gets tiny.

## What felt unfair
Nothing felt unfair. The bigger problem was that nothing felt dangerous. Stress stayed at 0/7 for every cook for four whole services. Once I had a third cook (Temp Cook) and Cast Iron (free rushes), the routine was the same every turn: plate, fire, rush, one cook runs the pass. I never saw a red ticket. I was waiting for the pressure the intro promised.

## What felt good
Clearing a 12-top while a VIP ticket sat on the rail. Popping the last plate and seeing "END TURN / FINISH SERVICE". The A+ stamp.

## 3 changes that would most raise my score
1. **Ramp the difficulty faster, or stop the shop from trivializing it.** Temp Cook plus Cast Iron made services 3-4 a cakewalk with zero stress. Make tickets arrive in bursts, add walk-in rushes, or make upgrades cost something real.
2. **Make "who can still act" obvious at a glance.** Grey out a spent cook's portrait, or put a big moves-left number on each face.
3. **Teach how to pick a cook in the first 10 seconds.** One line: "Tap a cook's face to pick them, then tap a station to walk there." Tapping a station to pick a cook only became clear after a few misfires.

Bonus: more variety in the twists, since a critic and a VIP were the only ones I met in four services.
