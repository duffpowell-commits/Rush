## Reviewer observations

**What worked:** Compelling action-budget puzzle. Expanding stations and crew, shop and
perks made progression engaging. Undo and burn warnings create confidence. No unfair
loss or crisis in this run.

**Three highest-impact changes:**

1. **Challenge and economy:** S2–4 all hit 100 despite imperfect routing. Jo and the trays
   were affordable early; four additional upgrades were bought after S2. Increase challenge
   and meaningful purchase tradeoffs for high-performing players without punishing beginners.
2. **Perk and stress clarity:** Runner's free action was unavailable after moving down to
   zero AP; perk wording suggests otherwise. “Skips rest” is ambiguous. Cast Iron's first
   rush is stress-free, but the initial action label displayed +1 stress; explain current
   cost and recovery precisely.
3. **Information hierarchy:** Tutorial and TABLE OUT/RUSHED overlays sometimes hid
   ticket/station status. +1 MORE hides information needed for demand planning. Prefer
   compact feedback outside gameplay information, and clear swipe/expand rail navigation.

## Recommended next milestone

Before advanced specialization content, improve clarity, add high-performance challenge
without weakening Jo or the early tutorial, and introduce distinct role choices with
tradeoffs. Retest with a new independent reviewer. The result is one skilled AI run;
it does not establish the experience of an actual human population.
