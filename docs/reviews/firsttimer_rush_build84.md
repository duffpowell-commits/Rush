# Rush first-timer review (build 84, live web, 390x780 touch)

**Score: 7/10.** Looks and sounds like a real game and service 1 teaches itself well, but service 2 jumped from easy to a pile-up I couldn't read, and I lost.

## How far I got
- Service 1: A (7 of 7 out, 0 walks, tips $50, 88 points). Bought SERVER TRAY ($30).
- Service 2 (new SAUTE station + VIP table): GAME OVER "THE WHOLE LINE WALKED OUT" at 12 tickets out. Never reached service 3 or tried RETRY (about 150 taps used).
- Why I lost: tickets stacked up faster than 2 cooks could handle. The grill sat unmanned with "2 TO FIRE" while I chased fry and saute. Food waited on the pass, burns forced refires, one table walked, then Dev's stress hit the cap and he quit, then Mo.

## What hooked me
- Title screen and kitchen art look polished and the cooks have faces. Real sizzle, printer and bell sounds make it feel alive.
- The coach hints ("TAP FIRE TO COOK") got me through the first turn without a tutorial wall.
- A big green "A" with "88 POINTS: out 60, speed 13, pans 15" felt rewarding. The shop has clear one-line effects.
- Server tray was a satisfying upgrade: "MO: RUN 2 PLATES" instantly felt better.
- Warnings like "PLATE NOW" and "END TURN / FRY BURNS", and "Word's out. Busier next service. Next: 9 tickets."

## What confused me
- Moving cooks: tap a face, then a station. The "WALK 1/2/3" and "TOO FAR" chips helped, but I still sent the wrong cook a few times, and a cook who finished sat where the other one needed to be.
- The small blue number badge (moves left) is easy to miss, so I didn't understand why a cook went "DONE" so quickly.
- Reading the ticket rail when busy: at FRY, cooking, on the pass, served, struck-through, plus "+1 MORE" paging. By service 2 I couldn't tell which dish needed me next.
- With 4 stations open and 2 cooks I got no signal about what to prioritise, so I neglected the grill.
- "IDLE COOKS LOSE STRESS" popped up while I was already underwater, too late to use.
- Stress sat at 0 to 1 all of service 1, then in service 2 went "2/6" to "MO QUITS" in about two turns. The button warning ("MO: FIRE T23 (MO QUITS)") is helpful but arrives after the damage is set up.
- Shop "COMP CARDS +1 TABLE CAN WALK" read like a penalty at first.

## Unfair or too easy
- Too easy: service 1 (7 tickets, never in danger). It never tested moving cooks or the pass.
- Unfair feeling: the jump to service 2. New station, VIP, 9 tickets, a BIG table of 4 dishes across two stations, and only $20 left after the tray.
- Once it slides there is no recovery: walked tables and quitting cooks snowball and the game ends quickly.
- Nothing told me a third cook (TEMP COOK $45) was the answer; I bought the tray because it was cheaper.

## Top 3 changes, ranked
1. Smooth the service 1 to 2 jump: introduce the new station or more tickets in smaller steps, or give a free helper in service 2, and hint toward TEMP COOK when the next service has more tickets than cooks can handle.
2. Make the ticket rail and stations scannable when busy: highlight the dish that burns or walks next, and show a marker on a station with waiting dishes and no cook nearby (for example "GRILL: 2 WAITING").
3. Show stress and quit risk earlier and more plainly: explain once, before it matters, that rushing and moving cost stress and tired cooks quit, and show it on the cook portrait while acting.

Minor: a one-tap "send cook to busiest station" shortcut would cut the face-then-station tapping, and COMP CARDS needs a clearer name.
