## Store / itch long description
Friday night. The rail fills up faster than you can read it.

Rush is a turn-based kitchen game for your phone. Each turn your two cooks get three moves: fire a dish, rush a pan, plate it on the pass, run it to the table. Every ticket is on a clock, and three walked tables ends the night.

- A new twist every service after the first: a bus tour, the grill on the fritz, a VIP, a food critic.
- The line grows: grill and fry, then saute, then a cold station.
- Bank your tips for upgrades that last the night.
- Grades from A+ to F, scored on tables out, speed and clean pans.
- Too hot? Set the PACE. Rough night? The kitchen eases off.

Free. No ads, no sign-up. Plays in a browser or on Android.
